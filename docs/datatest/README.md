# Test documents

Twenty fictional project documents to try the app with. Upload them one by one on the
**Add a project** page (or paste their text) and compare what the AI extracts with what the
document says.

They cover nine ASEAN countries and different document types – completion reports, annual and
quarterly reports, a mid-term review, proposals, a concept note, meeting minutes and an e-mail –
so the AI sees the same variety as in a real organization. All documents are in English.

**Try adding your own fields.** Every document contains more information than the nine default
fields collect, for example:

| Information in the documents | Field you could add on the *Set up your fields* page |
|---|---|
| Donor or funder | `donor` – "The organization that funds the project." |
| Total budget and currency | `budget` (number) – "The total project budget as a plain number." and `currency` – "The 3-letter currency code of the budget." |
| Number of beneficiaries | `beneficiaries` (number) – "The total number of people who benefit directly." |
| Partner organizations | `partners` (several values) – "Organizations that cooperate on the project, other than the implementing organization and the donor." |
| Project status | `status` – "Exactly one word: proposed, ongoing or completed." |
| Person responsible | `contact_person` – "The name of the project manager or contact person." |
| Document type | `document_type` – "The kind of document: proposal, progress report, completion report, meeting minutes or e-mail." |
| Lessons learned | `lessons` (several values) – "The main lessons learned, one short sentence each." |

Save the template, then extract a document again and see the new fields filled in.

All organizations, people, numbers and results are invented. Place names (towns, districts,
provinces) are real, so they can be found on the map.
