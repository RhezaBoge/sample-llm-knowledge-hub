## 1. Background

Northern Cebu was badly affected by Typhoon Odette in December 2021. Many school libraries lost their collections, and the three municipal libraries in the project area were closed for more than a year. A baseline survey carried out by the Bayanihan Reading Foundation in early 2024 found that only 18% of children aged 6–12 in the area had access to books outside school, and that reading comprehension scores in Grade 3 were below the provincial average in all three towns.

The Community Library Network project was designed with the Cebu Provincial Library to rebuild reading access through small, volunteer-run libraries located inside barangay halls, day-care centres and chapels, where families already gather.

## 2. Objectives

1. Establish ten community libraries in northern Cebu by May 2027.
2. Train a network of volunteer librarians and reading tutors.
3. Raise reading habits and comprehension among 5,000 children aged 4–14.
4. Hand over sustainable management to barangay councils and the Provincial Library.

## 3. Activities and results in Year 2

### 3.1 Libraries opened

Six libraries are now open (two per municipality), compared with the target of five by the end of Year 2. Each library has about 2,000 books, a children's reading corner, two tablets with offline reading apps, and solar back-up lighting.

### 3.2 Book collection

14,200 books were added in Year 2 (9,800 donated by publishers and schools in Cebu City, 4,400 purchased), bringing the total collection to 21,600. About 40% of the titles are in Cebuano, 45% in English and 15% in Filipino. All books are catalogued with an open-source library system, and the catalogue is shared between all six libraries.

### 3.3 Volunteers

48 volunteers completed the two-day training on early literacy, read-aloud techniques and library management (cumulative total: 84). Volunteers receive a small monthly transport allowance of PHP 1,200 and a certificate from the Provincial Library. Volunteer retention after 12 months is 79%.

### 3.4 Reading programmes

- 3,100 children hold a library card (62% of the Year 2 target of 5,000 by project end).
- The monthly "Story Saturday" session reached 2,870 children in Year 2.
- 380 children in Grades 2–3 joined a 10-week tutoring programme. Reading comprehension test scores improved by an average of 23 percentage points between pre- and post-tests.
- The "book bike" mobile library visited five barangays without a library every two weeks, lending 4,200 books.

### 3.5 Parents and community

Parent reading clubs started in four libraries with 210 members. A survey of 300 parents in March 2026 found that 71% now read with their children at least once a week, compared with 34% at baseline.

## 5. Challenges and lessons learned

- Two libraries opened later than planned because barangay halls needed roof repairs first. A small repair fund has been added to the Year 3 plan.
- Books in Cebuano are in short supply. The foundation is working with two local publishers to print 3,000 additional Cebuano picture books.
- Tablets are heavily used; a second tablet per library has been requested by volunteers.
- The book bike works well but depends on one volunteer driver. A second driver will be trained.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Typhoon damage to library buildings | Medium | Books stored in sealed boxes during typhoon season; insurance for equipment |
| Volunteer drop-out | Medium | Allowance, recognition events, pairing new volunteers with experienced ones |
| Barangay council change after 2025 elections | Low | Memoranda of agreement signed with councils, not individuals |

## 7. Plan for Year 3

Open four more libraries (San Remigio and Tabogon have asked to join), digitise the catalogue so that books can be reserved online, run the first "Northern Cebu Children's Book Festival" in November 2026, and complete the handover plan with the Provincial Library.
