# Community Coral Reef Monitoring, Nha Trang Bay

**Quarterly Project Update – Q3 2025**

*Fictional example document*

**Implementing organization:** Blue Reef Viet Nam
**Partners:** Nha Trang Bay Marine Protected Area Management Board; Institute of Oceanography, Nha Trang; Nha Trang Dive Operators Association
**Donor:** Coral Futures Initiative
**Country:** Viet Nam
**Location:** Hon Mun, Hon Tam and Hon Mot islands, Nha Trang Bay, Khanh Hoa Province
**Period:** 1 April 2024 – 31 March 2027
**Total budget:** USD 310,000
**Beneficiaries:** 54 trained reef monitors; 9 dive operators; 3 fishing communities (about 2,400 people); 3 secondary schools
**Status:** Ongoing
**Project manager:** Tran Minh Quang
**SDGs:** SDG 14: Life Below Water; SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth; SDG 13: Climate Action

## 1. Update summary

The project trains dive guides, fishers and students to monitor coral reef health on fixed transects, runs coral nurseries and transplants fragments onto degraded reef, and works with dive operators on reef-friendly practices. Live coral cover on restored plots has risen from 12% to 21% in 18 months, and nine dive operators have signed a reef-friendly code of conduct.

## 2. Context

Nha Trang Bay was Viet Nam's first marine protected area. Coral cover around Hon Mun declined sharply between 2015 and 2022 because of bleaching, crown-of-thorns starfish outbreaks, anchor damage and sediment from coastal construction. The MPA Management Board asked for community support with monitoring, which it could not carry out alone.

## 3. Progress this quarter

### Monitoring

- 54 trained reef monitors (22 dive guides, 18 fishers, 14 university students), of whom 41 were active this quarter.
- 18 fixed transects surveyed (quarterly survey no. 5 completed in August).
- Results: average live coral cover across all transects 23% (22% in Q2); crown-of-thorns density below the outbreak threshold on all transects; 2 bleaching alerts in July after the sea temperature exceeded 30°C for 10 days, with recovery by September.
- Data entered into the MPA database and shared with the Institute of Oceanography.

### Restoration

- 3,600 coral fragments grown in 4 nursery tables and transplanted onto 12 restoration plots (cumulative: 9,100 fragments).
- Survival of transplanted fragments after 12 months: 68%.
- Live coral cover on restoration plots: 21% (12% at baseline in April 2024).

### Dive operators

- 9 dive operators (of 14 in the bay) signed the reef-friendly code of conduct: no anchoring on reefs, mandatory guest briefing, no touching or feeding, maximum group size of 6 per guide.
- 11 mooring buoys installed with the MPA Board to replace anchoring at popular sites.
- A "reef-friendly operator" sticker scheme launched in July; early feedback from tourists is positive.

### Fishing communities

- Fishers from Bich Dam village started a seasonal no-take agreement for the Hon Mot reef (March–June), monitored by their own members.

## 4. Budget

Spent to date: USD 162,000 (52%).

## 5. Issues

- Two nursery tables were damaged by a storm in September; repairs cost USD 1,800 and were covered by the contingency line.
- Sediment from a construction site on the mainland affected one transect; the MPA Board has raised the issue with the provincial authorities.

## 6. Upcoming

- School programme with three secondary schools in Nha Trang starts in October 2025 (reef biology, snorkel survey for older students).
- First public "Reef Report Card" to be published in December 2025 and every six months thereafter.
- Training of 12 additional fisher monitors from Vung Ngan village in November.
