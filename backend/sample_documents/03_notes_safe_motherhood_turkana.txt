Hi all,

Quick notes from yesterday as promised (sorry for the mess!)

- SMT (Safe Motherhood Turkana) officially wrapped up end of June 2024. It started
  back in Jan 2021.
- Run by Afya Bora Partners, funding from the Northern Lights Health Foundation
  (EUR 480k total, final financial report due next month).
- We worked mainly out of Lodwar County Referral Hospital, with mobile outreach
  in Kakuma and Lokichoggio.
- Numbers: 6,200 pregnant women had at least 4 antenatal visits, 340 community
  health volunteers trained, total reach approx 15,000 women and newborns.
- Skilled birth attendance went from 38% to 61% in the target wards.
- Lessons: motorbike ambulances worked well but fuel costs were higher than
  planned. Radio messages in Ng'aturkana were very popular.

Next steps: share the final report with the County Health Department and
discuss a possible phase 2 in Kenya's other arid counties.
