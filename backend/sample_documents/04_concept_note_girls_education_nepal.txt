CONCEPT NOTE - DRAFT v2
(Fictional example)

Bright Futures: Keeping Girls in School in Karnali

Applicant: Himalayan Learning Network
Proposed donor: Open Classroom Trust
Proposed duration: 36 months (January 2027 - December 2029)
Requested amount: USD 265,000

Problem
In Karnali Province, Nepal, fewer than half of girls complete secondary school.
Long walking distances, household work, early marriage and a lack of private
toilets in schools are the main reasons girls drop out.

Approach
1. Scholarships and bicycles for 1,200 girls in grades 8-10 in Jumla and
   Kalikot districts.
2. Menstrual hygiene corners with water and private toilets in 30 schools.
3. Training for 150 teachers in Birendranagar (Surkhet) on inclusive,
   gender-responsive teaching.
4. Parent groups to discuss the value of girls' education and prevent early
   marriage.

Expected results
- 1,200 girls directly supported, 4,000 family members reached indirectly.
- Secondary completion rate for participating girls above 80%.

This concept note is proposed for review in the next funding round.
