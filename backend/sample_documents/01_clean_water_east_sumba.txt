PROJECT COMPLETION REPORT
(Fictional example)

Project title: Community Water Access and Hygiene for East Sumba
Implementing organization: Tirta Lestari Foundation
Donor: Blue Horizon Water Fund
Country: Indonesia
Reporting period: March 2022 - February 2025

1. Background
East Sumba Regency in East Nusa Tenggara province has one of the longest dry
seasons in Indonesia. Before the project, women and girls in many hamlets walked
up to 3 km every day to collect water, and school latrines were often unusable.

2. What we did
Between March 2022 and February 2025 the project built 14 rainwater harvesting
tanks and 6 solar-powered water pumps in three locations: the town of Waingapu,
the village of Kambera and the village of Lewa in Lewa sub-district.
Hygiene promotion sessions were run in 22 schools, and 42 school latrines were
rehabilitated. Each water point is managed by a village water committee with at
least 50% women members.

3. Results
In total 8,450 people (about 1,900 households) now have access to safe water
within 500 metres of their home. Diarrhoea cases reported at the Lewa health
post dropped by 31% compared to the baseline.

4. Budget
The total approved budget was USD 412,000, of which 96% was spent.

5. Status
The project closed on 28 February 2025. The water committees now manage and
maintain all systems with support from the district water office.
